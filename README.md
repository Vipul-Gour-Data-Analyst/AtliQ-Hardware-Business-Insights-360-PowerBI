# 📊 Business Insights 360 | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

**Business Insights 360** is an end-to-end Power BI analytics project designed to provide a **360° view of business performance** across Sales, Finance, Products, Customers, Markets, and Supply Chain.

The project analyzes a global computer and accessories business operating across **Asia Pacific, Latin America, North America, and Europe**, with sales generated through **Retailers, Direct Sales, and Distributors**.

The objective was to transform data from multiple business areas into **actionable insights** that help management understand performance, identify business risks, uncover growth opportunities, and make faster data-driven decisions.

---

# ⭐ Project Description — STAR Method

## 🟡 Situation

The company was experiencing challenges in understanding its global business performance. After entering the US market without sufficient data-driven market insights, the business faced financial losses.

As the company expanded across multiple regions and channels, decision-makers needed a centralized solution to monitor **sales, profitability, market share, customers, products, inventory, and forecasting performance**.

Existing reporting made it difficult to get a complete view of the business and quickly identify the reasons behind performance gaps.

## 🔵 Task

The goal was to develop an interactive **Business Insights 360 dashboard in Power BI** that brings together data from multiple business functions and provides management with a centralized view of key performance indicators.

The solution was designed to:

- Monitor overall business performance
- Analyze sales and profitability
- Identify regional and customer-level opportunities
- Evaluate product and inventory performance
- Track forecast accuracy and supply-chain risks
- Identify market-share and channel-related challenges
- Support data-driven product and expansion decisions

## 🟢 Action

I transformed the raw business data into an interactive Power BI analytics solution by:

- Cleaning and transforming data using **Power Query**
- Developing a structured **data model** for business analysis
- Creating calculated measures and KPIs using **DAX**
- Analyzing sales across **regions, customers, products, and channels**
- Evaluating **Net Sales, Gross Margin, Net Profit Margin, Forecast Accuracy, and Net Error**
- Analyzing product-level revenue and inventory risks
- Investigating **Out-of-Stock (OOS)** and **Excess Inventory (EI)** situations
- Creating interactive dashboards with filters, drill-downs, and business-focused visualizations
- Translating analytical findings into **business recommendations for management**

## 🔴 Result

The dashboard provided a centralized view of business performance and highlighted several important strategic areas:

- **$3.74B Net Sales**, only **1.86% below** the $3.81B benchmark
- **38.08% Gross Margin**, broadly aligned with the 38.34% benchmark
- **-13.98% Net Profit Margin**, highlighting profitability as a major concern
- **81.17% Forecast Accuracy**, showing improvement but with a **-$3.47M Net Error**
- **PC division contributed 61% of revenue**, indicating high business concentration
- **Retail contributed approximately 72% of revenue**, highlighting channel dependency
- PC market share declined from **7.63% to 5.87%**, indicating increasing competitive pressure
- **North America generated $1.02B revenue**, making it the strongest regional market
- India generated **$945M revenue** but showed significant profitability pressure
- **Notebooks generated approximately $1.58B revenue**, making them the leading product category

These insights helped identify opportunities around **market-share recovery, channel diversification, profitability improvement, inventory optimization, and more granular forecasting**.

# 📊 Dashboard Insights

## 1️⃣ Market Share & Channel Insights

### Key Insights

- 📉 **PC market share declined from 7.63% to 5.87%**, indicating increasing competitive pressure.
- 🛒 **Retail contributes approximately 71.5% of revenue**, creating significant channel dependency.
- 🎯 Direct Sales contribute **17.8%**.
- 📦 Distributors contribute **10.7%**.
- ⚔️ Competitors continue to capture a significant portion of the PC market.

### Strategic Recommendations

- Develop targeted product, pricing, and promotional strategies to recover market share.
- Strengthen **Direct and E-commerce channels** to reduce dependence on Retail.
- Benchmark competitors on pricing, product launches, promotions, and market positioning.
- Invest in high-growth and high-profitability segments.
- Develop differentiated pricing and incentive strategies across channels.

### Management Takeaway

> **Market share is declining while retail dependency remains high. Diversifying channels and strengthening competitive positioning are critical for sustainable growth.**

---

# 🌍 2️⃣ Regional & Customer Insights

### Key Insights

- 🇺🇸 **North America leads with $1.02B revenue**, making it the strongest regional market.
- 🇮🇳 **India generates $945M** but has significant profitability pressure.
- 🌏 **ROA contributes $789M** with comparatively better profitability.
- 🛒 **Amazon leads customers with approximately $497M in sales**, followed by AtliQ Exclusive and AtliQ e Store.
- ⚠️ Several important customer accounts show forecasting and net-error challenges.

### Strategic Recommendations

- Prioritize high-value customers through targeted pricing and retention strategies.
- Improve India's profitability through **pricing optimization, product-mix improvement, and cost control**.
- Diversify the customer base by developing mid-tier accounts.
- Reduce dependency on a small number of major customers.
- Implement customer-level profitability monitoring.

### Management Takeaway

> **North America drives revenue, while India requires urgent profitability improvement. Customer diversification and account-level analytics can reduce concentration risk.**

---

# 💻 3️⃣ Product & Inventory Insights

### Key Insights

- 💻 **Notebooks lead with approximately $1.58B revenue**.
- 🔌 Peripherals generate approximately **$898M**.
- 🖥️ Desktops generate approximately **$711M**.
- 🎧 Accessories contribute approximately **$454M**.
- 🔴 Several high-demand products face **Out-of-Stock (OOS)** risks.
- 🎯 Batteries, External SSDs, and Graphics Cards show weaker forecast accuracy.
- 📦 Selected SKUs have **Excess Inventory (EI)** risks, increasing working-capital requirements.

### Strategic Recommendations

- Prioritize inventory for high-demand Notebook, Desktop, and Peripheral SKUs.
- Reduce excess stock through targeted promotions and product bundles.
- Strengthen replenishment strategies for critical products.
- Implement SKU-level forecasting for volatile products.
- Introduce **ABC inventory classification** to prioritize high-value products.

### Management Takeaway

> **The priority is to keep high-demand products available while reducing excess inventory to balance availability, working capital, and profitability.**

---

# 📈 4️⃣ Forecasting & Supply Chain

### Key Insights

- 📊 **Forecast Accuracy improved to 81.17%**.
- 🔴 **Net Error increased to -$3.47M**, indicating persistent negative forecasting bias.
- 📉 Absolute Net Error improved by approximately **29%**.
- ⚠️ Forecast accuracy varies significantly across customers and products.

### Strategic Recommendations

- Investigate the root causes of persistent negative forecast bias.
- Implement **customer + SKU-level forecasting**.
- Conduct monthly **Forecast vs Actual** reviews.
- Create automated alerts for customers and SKUs below accuracy thresholds.
- Integrate **Sales + Inventory + Forecast** data into a unified demand-planning process.

### Management Takeaway

> **Forecast accuracy is improving, but persistent negative bias creates supply-chain risk. Granular forecasting and continuous monitoring can help close the gap.**

# 💡 Key Business Takeaways

The analysis revealed that the business has **strong revenue generation but faces profitability and sustainability challenges**.

The most important opportunities identified were:

1. **Improve profitability**, particularly in markets such as India.
2. **Recover lost market share** through better pricing, product positioning, and promotions.
3. **Reduce channel dependency** by strengthening Direct and E-commerce sales.
4. **Optimize inventory** by reducing excess stock and preventing stock-outs.
5. **Improve forecast quality** through customer- and SKU-level forecasting.
6. **Diversify the customer base** to reduce concentration risk.
7. Focus investment on **high-growth and high-profitability products and markets**.
